About the app

Vedic Clock — Panchang is a screensaver-style Vedic clock that shows the current time, traditional Vedic time (Ghati, Pala, Vipala), and the daily Panchang (Tithi, Vara, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana) along with festivals and auspicious timings for your location. Everything is calculated on your device and works fully offline.

Frequently asked questions

The clock says “Set Location.” What do I do?
Open Settings (the gear icon), then tap Detect to use your current location, or type your city into the search box. Panchang timings need a location to be accurate.

How do I switch between English and Hindi?
Tap the अ / A button at the top right of the main screen, or change the Language in Settings.

Can I change the look?
Yes. In Settings you can pick the Traditional or Modern Dark theme, choose a devotional art or solid-colour background, and toggle festivals and extra details on or off.

Why do some festival dates differ by a day from another calendar?
Festival and Panchang dates are computed on the device using an astronomical model. Occasionally a date may differ by a day from a regional almanac due to local conventions. Always confirm important dates with your local Panchang.

Is my data private?
Yes. The app collects no personal data and has no servers. See our Privacy Policy.
