Privacy Policy

Vedic Clock — Panchang · Last updated: 19 September 2026

Vedic Clock — Panchang (“the app”) is designed to respect your privacy. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no backend server. All features run entirely on your device.

Location

If you grant location permission, the app uses your device’s location only on the device to calculate accurate sunrise, sunset, and Panchang timings for your area. Your location is never transmitted off the device and is never sent to us or any third party. You may instead search for a city manually. You can revoke location access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Data storage

Your preferences (such as chosen location, language, theme, and display options) are stored locally on your device only. Removing the app deletes this data.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. It makes no network requests to our servers.

Children’s privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
